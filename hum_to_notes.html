How it works

Free accounts see an on-screen preview. Premium unlocks the full chart and the downloads.

  1. Log in on the main page first.
  2. Record or upload. Press Record, then sing or whistle one note at a time and stay close to the mic. Press Stop when you're done. Or upload a recording of your voice.
  3. Wait a moment. The notes appear on their own, with the tempo and key. There is no extra button to press.
  4. Download (Premium). Press Download MIDI or Download MusicXML. The name you typed becomes the file name.
  5. Open it in FL Studio. Open an empty Piano roll and drag the .mid file into it. In the import window tick the track and press Accept, then pick any instrument to hear it.

Tips for better results

Clean input gives clean notes.

Keep it quiet

Record in a quiet room with no music playing behind you.

One note at a time

Use "doo" or "la" so each note has a clear start. Slide between notes as little as you can.

Short and steady

Keep a steady pace and stay under a minute. Short phrases give the cleanest result.

Questions

 

Does it work with more than one voice?

No. It follows a single melody line. Chords, harmonies and backing music will confuse it.

Can I use a full song?

You can, but the result will be rough. It follows a single melody line, so drums, chords and backing music add wrong notes. For a real song, split it into stems on the main page first, then use just the vocal or bass stem.

Why are some notes wrong?

Notes are detected automatically from the sound of your voice, so a wobbly pitch or a noisy room can cause mistakes. Treat the result as a first draft and fix anything that sounds off.

Can I use this for free?

Yes. Free accounts see the first few measures on screen. Premium unlocks the full chart and the MIDI and MusicXML downloads.

What can I open the downloads in?

MIDI opens in music production programs. MusicXML opens in notation software such as MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius and Dorico.
