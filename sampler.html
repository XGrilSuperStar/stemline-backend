How it works

  1. Pick a saved song and a stem, then press Load stem. Only your own saved stems appear here.
  2. Drag across the waveform to grab a piece. Pull the edges to fine-tune it, and scroll to zoom in for precise cuts.
  3. Click a pad, then Save to selected pad. The sample keeps a little extra audio on each side, so you can re-trim it later.
  4. Play pads by clicking them or with the keys 1 2 3 4 / Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V.
  5. Everything you save here is also waiting on the mixer: the stem's colored pad plays it, and it shows up in the mixer's SAMPLES list with its own play, headphones, trim and delete buttons.
  6. Use Headphones to send a pad to a second output so you can audition it before the room hears it.
  7. Samples are stored in this browser. Premium accounts can download any sample as an MP3, which makes a handy ringtone.

HOW TO SAMPLE

  1. Pick a saved song and a stem, then press Load stem.
  2. Drag across the waveform to grab a piece. Pull the edges to adjust it, and scroll to zoom in.
  3. Click a pad, give the sample a name, then press Save to selected pad.
  4. Click a pad, or press its key, to play the sample back.
  5. In the Pad editor, drag the edges to re-trim, rename it, change volume or pitch, or set it to loop.
  6. The headphones button plays a pad in headphones only, so the room never hears it.
  7. Your samples also appear on the mixer: the stem's colored pad plays it, and the SAMPLES list holds the rest.
