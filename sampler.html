How it works

  1. Load a stem. Pick one of your saved songs, pick a stem (vocals, drums...), press Load stem.
  2. Listen. Press Preview. With nothing selected it plays the whole stem from wherever you last clicked. Press it again to stop.
  3. Grab a piece. Drag across the waveform. Pull the two orange edges to fine-tune. Scroll wheel zooms; to move left/right, drag the time ruler along the top (or hold Shift and scroll).
  4. Pick a pad. Click any pad in the Pads grid.
  5. Save. Type a name and press Save to selected pad. The pad lights up. Repeat steps 3-5 for more pads.
  6. Play it. Click the pad, or press its key (1 2 3 4 / Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V).
  7. Edit it. Click a saved pad and the Sample editor opens: re-trim, rename, Loop, and turn the Volume and Pitch knobs by dragging them up or down (double-click to reset).
  8. Use it on the main mixer. Saved samples are already there: the stem's colored pad plays it, and the mixer's SAMPLES list has play, headphones and delete. The channel's volume and mute control it, and Stop or the spacebar silences it.
  9. Headphones. Use the Headphones button to hear a pad privately before the room does.
  10. Samples live in this browser. Premium can download a sample as an MP3 (great for ringtones).
