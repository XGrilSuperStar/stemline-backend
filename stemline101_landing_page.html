STM-001 5-CHANNEL DECONSTRUCTION UNIT

Split any song into stems — clean vocals, drums, bass, and more — then blend them with stems from other songs on one mixer. A song stem splitter for DJing, ringtones, and learning how to make a remix.

01 —

What's a stem?

One isolated part of a song — just the vocals, just the drums, just the bass — pulled out from the full mix so you can work with it on its own.

02 —

Drop

Drop any MP3 or WAV into the box below and Stemline101 pulls it apart automatically, no editing experience needed.

03 —

Splits into stems

The track separates into five clean stems: vocals, drums, bass, guitar, and everything else, ready to use individually.

04 —

Load onto channels

Browse your library and load any stem from any song onto a channel, mixing and matching pieces from completely different tracks.

05 —

Saved to library

Save your loaded setup to your library so it's ready to pick back up anytime, no re-splitting required.

06 —

Mix on the board

Blend, adjust, and play with every stem live on real mixer controls, building something new out of parts that never originally belonged together.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH STEMS
Remove vocals
Make an instrumental for karaoke or practice, or keep the vocal on its own as an acapella.
Make a mashup
Put a vocal from one song over the drums and bass of another, matched in tempo and key.
DJ with stems
Bass swaps, acapella layers, and drum loops for smoother transitions and bigger builds.
Sample
Pull a clean drum break or vocal phrase out of a song and trim it for your own tracks.
Practice along
Mute your own instrument, slow hard parts down, and play with the original band.
Make a ringtone
Choose the best 20 to 30 seconds of a song and shape it into a ringtone.
